# The Spreadsheet Blueprint

This guide explains how to use our local tool and skill tracker without tracking private data or putting price tags on neighborhood help. 

By keeping our formatting simple, we ensure these files match the rules of The Neighborhood Index so that any block can easily manage its own resources.

## Column Rules

* **Household ID:** Use simple codes like H01 or H02. Do not write down specific names, phone numbers, or exact street addresses on this shared file.
* **Resource Type:** Put items into basic categories like tools, skills, space, or care networks.
* **Specific Description:** Write down what the resource actually is, like a 10 foot ladder, a chainsaw, an open garage for car repairs, or backup emergency childcare.
* **How to Contact:** Use direct, local methods that do not rely on apps or corporate links. You can write simple notes like knock on side door or drop a text.
* **Last Checked:** Note when the information was updated. We typically review this list twice a year, once during spring yard cleanups and once right before winter storms hit.

## Safety Rules

* **No price tags.** Never write down how much an item or a favor is worth in money. 
* **No tabs.** Never track who owes what to whom. All sharing on our blocks is completely voluntary, and strong relationships are our actual wealth.
* **Keep it offline.** Keep this spreadsheet stored locally on your own home computer or print it out on a physical sheet of paper. Do not upload it to public cloud links or online mapping software where companies can scrape our personal details.
