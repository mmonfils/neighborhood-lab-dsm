# Neighborhood Lab DSM

## 1. What This Project Is
* **A Macro Social Work Infrastructure:** An open-source research and operational hub dedicated to mapping, analyzing, and organizing the local dynamics of Des Moines, Iowa.
* **Theoretical Framework:** Built entirely through the lenses of democratic federalism (decentralizing authority) and Asset-Based Community Development (ABCD) (focusing on local wealth, skills, and resources).
* **The Mission:** To translate complex, scattered public policy and economic data into plain-language, actionable frameworks that everyday neighbors can use to advocate for themselves and build resilient street-level networks.

---

## 2. The Core Problem & Our Approach
* **The Problem:** Crucial data regarding zoning laws, regional economics, urban planning, and local history is buried inside dense municipal portals or isolated within academic silos. This structure keeps residents in the dark about changes impacting their own backyards.
* **The Shift:** We reject top-down bureaucratic interventions. This repository functions as a dual-engine translation layer—bridging macro-level institutional frameworks with decentralized, street-level execution.

---

## 3. Global Repository Mapping

### Part I: The Research Desk (Macro-Policy & Regional Data)
* Houses structural frameworks, systemic data tracking, and institutional analysis matching the Substack **Lab Notes** focus.
* `/policy-analysis/`: Contains deep dives into regional public policy, law, and history, anchoring our framework to counter corporate and institutional capture.
* `/datasets/`: Houses standardized, geocoded civic assets—including the Des Moines Public Library (DMPL) community resources layout—reformatted into flat, accessible spreadsheet files to bypass advanced technical barriers.

### Part II: The Action Folder (Hyper-Local Block Infrastructure)
* Located entirely inside `/the-block-network/` to serve as our street-level coordination engine. 
* Houses the localized manuals, mutual aid frameworks, and decentralized templates that model backyard-fence, community-casual organizing for individual city blocks.

---

## 4. Operational Assets
* **`data-resources.csv`:** Centralized and formatted regional data collections structured for straightforward community access.
* **`block-toolkit-template.csv`:** A practical inventory template used by residents to chart shared skills, physical tools, and community spaces on a single street grid.
* **`neighborhood-exchange-manual.md`:** The operational handbook for deploying a localized, time-banking-inspired system to swap neighbor favors without cash changing hands.

---
