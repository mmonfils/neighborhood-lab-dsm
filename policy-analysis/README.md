# The Anti-Powell Framework: Democratizing Power & Wealth

## 1. What This Framework Is
* **The Strategic Shift:** In 1971, the Lewis Powell Memo outlined a highly organized, top-down corporate takeover of American laws, courts, universities, and media. This framework is its direct opposite.
* **Our Core Strategy:** Instead of consolidating corporate power from the top down, we use democratic federalism to push decision-making authority down to the block level. We use Asset-Based Community Development (ABCD) to build economic resilience using local resources already present right here in Des Moines.
* **The Lab Notes Lens:** This folder functions as our policy analysis engine room, stripping away dense academic jargon to show how street-level organizing counters systemic corporate capture.

---

## 2. Pillar 1: Neutralizing Corporate Information Control
* **The Old Problem:** Corporate-funded think tanks and specialized lobbying groups write complex public policies behind closed doors, leaving local neighborhoods completely out of the process.
* **Our Local Action:** * We fund and elevate community-born knowledge through an open-source, digital neighborhood commons.
  * We translate complex regional economic trends, local zoning codes, and municipal planning decisions into direct, plain-language insights.
  * This matches **The Porch** philosophy, ensuring everyday Des Moines residents can read, understand, and safely challenge institutional decisions impacting their backyards.

---

## 3. Pillar 2: Insulating Public Choice from Big Money
* **The Old Problem:** Policies like *Citizens United* treat corporate spending as protected speech, allowing outside capital to dominate local political landscapes and dictate municipal priorities.
* **Our Local Action:**
  * We establish absolute economic reciprocity through non-market networks that completely bypass corporate systems.
  * By building localized exchange networks, we treat every single resident’s hour as an asset of equal structural value, whether that hour is spent mapping zoning policy or shoveling a neighboring driveway.
  * This structural insulation preserves the block’s internal wealth and prevents corporate or political organizations from buying or gatekeeping local community support.

---

## 4. Pillar 3: Opening Up the Legal and Judicial System
* **The Old Problem:** The legal system has been systematically weaponized into an expensive, jargon-filled barrier designed to protect accumulated property and business interests over human needs.
* **Our Local Action:**
  * We build local legal literacy by cataloging municipal codes, neighborhood histories, and administrative rules into open, shared datasets.
  * We implement a highly strict, flat-hierarchy model of distributed leadership. By creating self-governing street networks with mandatory role rotations, we stop professionalized hierarchies from forming.
  * This shifts the balance of power, ensuring everyday Central Iowa residents possess the transparent information and organized local networks needed to present structural facts directly to city hall.
