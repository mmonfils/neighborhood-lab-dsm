# Keeping Power Local

This section traces how centralized money and corporate lobbying groups shape our local laws, and how communities can use local decision-making to balance that pressure.

## Core Points

* Corporate groups historically organized top-down networks to change schools, courts, and regional laws.
* Our response is to focus entirely on community-controlled knowledge and independent local groups.
* Decisions should always be made at the most local level possible, right where people live.
* We treat local neighborhoods as places that already have deep strengths, rather than places that need to be fixed by outside institutions.
