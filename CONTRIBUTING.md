# Guidance on How to Help

Neighborhood Lab DSM is an open experiment. You do not need to be a data scientist or a historian to help. You just need to care about your block.

## How You Can Contribute

1. **Tracking the Blueprint:** If you are tracking down how a macro policy, a financial shift, state law, or corporate funding networks are pressuring central Iowa, add your plain-language write-ups or timelines to our `tracking-the-blueprint/` directory.
2. **Organize Your Street:** Use the practical guides and templates inside `front-porch-manual/` to start a conversation and map out shared resources right on your own block grid. 
3. **Refine the Tools:** If you find a cleaner way to format our local spreadsheet templates, a better casual alternative for a column header, or want to improve our step-by-step organizing guides, share your ideas with us.

## How the Process Works

* **For Neighbors:** If you are not familiar with GitHub or code tools, do not sweat it. You can simply click the Issues tab on this page and open a new discussion to share a local story, a community need, or a suggestion.
* **For Researchers and Builders:** Feel free to fork this repository, add your local research or asset templates to the right folder, and submit a pull request. Make sure all spreadsheet data files use user-friendly layouts (no underscores in column headers, clear words with spaces) so any neighbor can comfortably open them locally in Excel.

## Our Shared Work

By contributing to this project, you agree that your work will be released entirely into the public domain under our CC0 License. This keeps our neighborhood tools completely free for everyone to use forever, with no corporate strings attached.
