# Guidance on How to Help

Neighborhood Lab DSM is an open experiment. You don't need to be a data scientist or a historian to help; you just need to care about your block.

## How You Can Contribute
1. **Research and History:** If you find a historical photo, property map, or regional policy document relevant to Des Moines development, add it to our `/history/` or `/maps/` directories.
2. **Deploy Block Infrastructure:** Use the resources inside [`/the-block-network/`](./the-block-network/) to launch a hyper-local network on your street. 
3. **Refine the Tools:** If you build a digital implementation of **The Block Toolkit** or optimize **The Neighborhood Exchange** workflows, submit an improvement.

## The Process
* **For Neighbors:** If you aren't familiar with GitHub, you can simply "Open an Issue" on this page to share a local story, an asset map, or a suggestion.
* **For Researchers/Advocates:** Feel free to "Fork" this repository, add your regional data or frameworks to the appropriate folder, and submit a "Pull Request."

## Rights
By contributing to this project, you agree that your work will be released into the Public Domain under our **CC0 License**. This ensures our neighborhood tools remain free for everyone in Des Moines to use forever.
