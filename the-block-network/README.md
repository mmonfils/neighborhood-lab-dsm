# The Front Porch Manual 🏡

Welcome to the backyard engine room of the block network. While the root of this project keeps track of big-picture county frameworks, law, and regional policy, this folder is where our boots hit the pavement. 

This manual is the master anchor for your street. It coordinates four simple, balanced pillars designed to give everyday neighbors the power to map their own community wealth, look out for one another during emergency winter storms, and share tools without money or tracking debt.

---

## 🧭 The Four Pillars of the Block

To turn a standard street grid into a resilient, self-governing mini-republic, we coordinate our efforts across four hyper-local files in this folder:

* **1. The Street Guide (`the-street-guide.md`)**
    * **What it is:** Our hyper-local playbook.
    * **The Goal:** It applies Occam's razor to block organizing, giving everyday folks a brief, clear map to host their first porch meeting and navigate neighborhood boundaries without bureaucratic friction.
* **2. The Neighborhood Toolkit (`the-neighborhood-toolkit.md`)**
    * **What it is:** The physical asset tracker.
    * **The Goal:** Replaces depressing, deficit-based agency "needs assessments" with a strength-focused inventory (built on `block-toolkit-template.csv`). It lists who owns snowblowers, who has specialized medical training, who has a backup generator, and who can offer an hour of emergency childcare.
* **3. The Neighborhood Exchange (`the-neighborhood-exchange.md`)**
    * **What it is:** Our non-monetized reciprocity protocol.
    * **The Goal:** A simple tracking layout (using `exchange-log-template.csv`) for favor-trading, lawnmower-lending, and mutual aid. It is strictly cash-free to insulate backyard relationships from market exploitation and commercial pricing rules.
* **4. The Block Roster (`the-block-roster.md`)**
    * **What it is:** The distributed leadership log.
    * **The Goal:** An egalitarian "chore wheel" that coordinates block tasks and communication. Roles and access permissions rotate automatically every quarter so that nobody becomes a permanent gatekeeper, keeping our local democracy flat and shared.

---

## 🛠️ How to Keep the Toolkit Running Smoothly

* **Equal Dignity Ledger:** On our block, all contributions hold identical value. A neighbor lending an expensive power tool and a neighbor spending an hour checking in on an elderly resident during an Iowa blizzard are recorded with equal respect. No dollar signs allowed.
* **Strict Local Privacy:** Keep these toolkit rosters and exchange logs completely offline or shared only via secure, direct spreadsheets among participating homes. Do not upload active personal phone numbers or specific tool maps to public web databases.
* **Seasonal Porch Tuning:** Update the asset inventory rows at least twice a year—ideally during spring cleanup and right before the winter freeze—to ensure the block knows exactly what localized infrastructure is standing ready.
