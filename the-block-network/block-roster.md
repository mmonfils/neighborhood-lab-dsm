# The Block Roster 📋
*The Distributed Leadership & Rotation Log*

## 1. Core Philosophy: The Flat Mini-Republic
The Block Roster is our primary defense framework against institutional capture and bureaucratic creep. To honor the principles of democratic federalism at the street level, authority must remain decentralized and temporary. 

* **No Permanent Heads:** We reject top-down leadership models. No single resident or outside professional acts as the permanent "director" or gatekeeper of this block.
* **Shared Stewardship:** Leadership is reframed as a predictable, shared chore—much like taking turns clear-cutting snow from a shared alleyway or maintaining a neighborhood walk-along.
* **Low Stakes, High Visibility:** By standardizing the rotation of simple roles, we build local civic capacity without burning out individual neighbors.

---

## 2. Core Operational Roles
To keep the block network operational, self-reliant, and organized, we recognize three simple, non-hierarchical roles that rotate regularly:

* **The Porch Host (Logistics)**
  * Responsible for providing a space (front porch, driveway, or backyard patio) for seasonal check-ins and asset verification.
  * Ensures that a physical phone tree or basic printed flyer map is distributed to participating houses.
* **The Ledger Keeper (Data Continuity)**
  * Responsible for maintaining the offline, flat spreadsheets (`block-toolkit-template.csv` and `exchange-log-template.csv`).
  * Manages updates when neighbors add a new tool, change a contact handle, or report a completed service exchange.
* **The Welcome Wagon (Relational Outreach)**
  * Serves as the primary point of contact to welcome new families moving onto the physical street footprint.
  * Drops off a plain-language copy of **The Street Guide** and introduces them to the concept of the local asset pool.

---

## 3. Anti-Hierarchy Guardrails
To prevent the concentration of control and ensure long-term community resilience, every participating block enforces these three structural rules:

* **Mandatory Rotation:** Roles must rotate at fixed intervals—specifically every quarter (January, April, July, and October). No resident may hold the same operational role for consecutive terms.
* **Radical Transparency:** The active roster, the shared asset toolkit index, and the service exchange log must remain fully visible and open to every participating home on the block grid.
* **Sovereign Boundaries:** This roster is maintained locally and privately by the block. It is never uploaded to city municipal portals, shared with external non-profit databases, or handed over to institutional registries.

## 🪵 Maintenance Protocol
* **The Quarterly Handover:** On the final Saturday of each quarter, the outgoing Ledger Keeper passes the local project folder (containing the CSV files and printed guides) to the incoming volunteer.
* **The Asset Verification Sync:** During the handover, the incoming Porch Host coordinates a casual, face-to-face check-in with neighbors to verify that contact channels and winter/summer emergency assets remain accurate.
