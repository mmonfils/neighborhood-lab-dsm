# The Blueprint Tracker

Use this straightforward template to document and break down big city hall plans, regional developmental changes, or top-down rule shifts moving through central Iowa. This framework helps neighbors cut through public marketing sales pitches to see exactly how a change trickles down to our local streets.

### 1. The Pressure Point
*   **The Big Shift:** What specific top-down rule change, corporate planning shift, or municipal proposal is currently moving through city hall?
*   **The Local Groundwork:** How did our streets handle this specific issue historically before the new rules were introduced? Focus on the structural facts rather than political theories.

### 2. The Behind-the-Scenes Map
*   **The Driving Interests:** Who is funding or actively pushing this change? Identify the corporate or outside entities backing the proposal.
*   **The Hidden Changes:** Look past the public promotional marketing. What is the actual shift in local control or resource distribution written into the fine print?
*   **The Exposed Doors:** Which specific households, block grids, or neighborhood lots will feel the immediate brunt of this shift?

### 3. The Everyday Impact
*   **The Block Reality:** How does this macro shift change physical land use, alter everyday household budgets, or shift community self-reliance on an Iowa grid?
*   **The Household Status:** Does this change require neighbors to give up local data, rely more on corporate cloud connections, or alter their home security?

### 4. The Porch Shield
*   **Clear, Simple Facts:** What objective data points or spreadsheet fields do neighbors need to keep in their local records to negotiate effectively?
*   **Defensive Blocks:** What independent tracking systems or sharing practices can neighbors deploy immediately to give their streets a buffer and work things out together?
