# Big Picture Framework

Use this template to break down outside developments, policy shifts, or institutional messaging so the block can look past the noise and figure out what it actually means for our neighborhood.

---

## 1. The Big Picture Challenge
*   **What is the outside shift or proposal called?** (e.g., regional zoning updates, overhauls to commuter routes, outside sprawl from large organizations).
*   **Where is this coming from?** (e.g., city hall, outside development groups, regional planning authorities).
*   **What is the official timeline or target date?** 

---

## 2. The Push and the Messaging
*   **What is the public sales pitch or narrative being used?** (List the specific buzzwords, slogans, or promotional talking points being shared in official notices or media).
*   **What problem do they claim this will solve for us?**
*   **What are they leaving out?** (Note the gaps between their official promises and the actual realities on our streets).

---

## 3. The Play for Our Blocks
*   **How does this directly impact our local self-reliance?** (Will this alter our shared public resources, affect neighbor privacy, or change who controls the immediate space?).
*   **What are the early warning signs to look out for on our streets?** 
*   **What simple, proactive steps can independent neighbors take early to protect their block?**
