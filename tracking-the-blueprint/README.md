# Tracking the Blueprint

This folder serves as an independent community research space used to break down big city hall plans, regional funding shifts, and changing town rules into clear, everyday facts. By tracking how top-down money, laws, and policy shifts trickle down to central Iowa streets, neighbors can insulate their streets early and keep decision-making power on the front porch.

## Active Research Workbenches
*   `blueprint-tracker.md`: A simple, step-by-step markdown template neighbors can use to study top-down rules and document their direct, block-level everyday impacts.
