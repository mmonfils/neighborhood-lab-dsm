# Guidance on How to Help

Neighborhood Lab DSM is an open experiment. You don't need to be a data scientist or a historian to help; you just need to care about your block.

## How You Can Contribute
1.  **Map Your Assets:** Use our Resource Map templates to document the "Favors" and "Strengths" of your specific neighborhood.
2.  **Share Local History:** Found a historical photo or an old policy document about Des Moines? Add it to our `/history/` folder.
3.  **Refine the Toolkit:** If you use the **Favor Bank** and find a way to make it work better, let us know! 

## The Process
* **For Neighbors:** If you aren't familiar with GitHub, you can simply "Open an Issue" on this page to share a story, a map, or a suggestion.
* **For Researchers/Advocates:** Feel free to "Fork" this repository, add your data or documents to the appropriate folder, and submit a "Pull Request."

## Rights
By contributing to this project, you agree that your work will be released into the Public Domain under our **CC0 License**. This ensures our neighborhood tools remain free for everyone in Des Moines to use forever.
